Where they were born

Queen Victoria reigned over Britain for 63 years. Of her 107 ancestors in this chart with a known birthplace, 96 were born in what is now Germany.

Turn on Atlas and every ancestor is colored by birthplace, with a legend that counts them. Gildroot recognizes about 600 place names: countries in English and in their own spelling, old kingdoms, US states, Canadian provinces and Irish counties.

When it can't place a birthplace, it asks you instead of guessing:

Questions

Before you start

Where do I get a family tree file?

Most family tree websites and programs can save your tree as a GEDCOM file, which ends in .ged. On Ancestry, open your tree, choose Tree Settings, then Export tree. Our export guides show the steps for 8 websites and programs. No file at all? Build your chart from what you know in about 3 minutes.

Is my family tree uploaded anywhere?

No. Your file is read by your own browser, on your own computer. Nothing from it is sent to us. You can check: load the page, turn off your wifi, then open your file. It still works.

How big can I print, and how many generations fit?

Up to 6 generations on Letter or A4 (5 in the free PDF), 6 on 11×14, 7 on 18×24 and 8 on 24×36 inches. We suggest the size that shows your tree best, and tell you where to print it the same day.

What if a name or a place comes out wrong?

Click it and fix it on the chart. Your original file is never changed. Names in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hebrew or Arabic script can't be printed yet. We'll show you which ones before you buy.
