What people ask before they start

How do I get my family tree file out of Ancestry?

Open your tree on Ancestry, click the tree’s name at the top left, and choose Tree settings. On the right, click Export tree. When it has finished, click Download your GEDCOM file. It saves to your Downloads folder, ready to drop in here.

Our export guides show each step with pictures, for Ancestry, MyHeritage, FamilySearch and five other programs.

Is my family tree uploaded anywhere?

No. Gildroot reads your file inside your web browser, on your own computer or iPad. Names, dates and places are never sent to us or to anyone else. After your first visit it works with wifi off, so you can check for yourself.

My tree has gaps. Will the chart look unfinished?

No. A missing ancestor shows as a faint empty space, the way printed genealogy charts have always done it, or you can trim empty branches away. Unknown dates are simply left out. If you only know a few people, the chart uses a layout made for small families.

Will names with accents, Greek or Cyrillic letters print correctly?

Yes. Seán, Dvořák, Ødegård, Nguyễn, Παπαδόπουλος and Иванова all print in the chart’s own typeface.

Names in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hebrew or Arabic script can’t be printed yet. We’ll show you which ones before you buy, and you can type a romanized form for each.
