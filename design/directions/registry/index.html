Method3steps

From your file to your wall.

No account, no retyping. It uses the research you have already done.

  1. Step1

    Export your tree

    Your family tree program saves a copy of your tree as a GEDCOM file. On Ancestry, open your tree, choose Tree Settings, then Export tree. MyHeritage and FamilySearch have their own steps.

    Export guides for 8 programs

  2. Step2

    Pick a chart and a style

    A fan of everyone who came before. Two families side by side, for a wedding or an anniversary. Or a classic pedigree. Then one of six styles.

    Spot a wrong date? Fix it on the chart. Your file is never changed.

  3. Step3

    Print it today

    Download a print-ready PDF for any copy shop, a JPEG for a photo counter, or print it at home across Letter sheets and tape them together.

    The bigger the paper, the more generations fit.

Questions4asked first

Questions people ask first.

Where do I find my GEDCOM file?

It is a copy of your tree that your family tree program makes when you choose Export. The file name ends in .ged. On Ancestry, open your tree, choose Tree Settings, then Export tree, and download the file when it is ready. We have step-by-step guides with pictures for 8 programs.

Is my family tree uploaded anywhere?

No. Your file is read by the web browser on your own computer. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no account. After your first visit, Gildroot works with the wifi turned off, so you can check this yourself.

How big can I print it, and how many generations fit?

Up to 24×36 inches (or A1), which holds 8 generations on a fan chart. Letter or A4 holds 6, and 18×24 holds 7. Gildroot shows you what fits before you pay.

Some names are in Greek or Cyrillic. Will they print?

Yes. Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and Vietnamese letters all print, with their accents: Dvořák, Ødegård, Nguyễn, Παπαδόπουλος, Иванова. Names in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hebrew or Arabic script can't be printed yet. We'll show you which ones before you buy.
