How to read a fan chart

1 2 3
Ahnentafel numbers. Victoria is 1. A father is always double his child’s number, a mother double plus one.
II–VII
Generations. Each ring holds twice the one inside it: 2 parents, 4 grandparents, up to 64.
3
Pedigree collapse. The dot counts how often someone appears. Ernest I, Duke of Saxe-Gotha appears 3 times.

Point at any name on the chart to trace the line back to Victoria.

QuestionsMore answers on the help page

Before you start.

Where do I get a family tree file?

From the site or program where you keep your tree. On Ancestry, open your tree, choose Tree settings, then Export tree. On MyHeritage, choose Family tree, Manage trees, then Export to GEDCOM. You get one file that ends in .ged. Our export guides show every click for 8 sites and programs.

Is my family tree uploaded anywhere?

No. Your own browser reads the file, on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded. You can check it yourself: open the studio, turn off wifi, and drop in your file. It still works.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once, from $29, and you can re-export your chart in any size and style for as long as you like. If it isn’t right for you, we refund you within 30 days, no questions asked.

Will names with accents or other alphabets print?

Accents, Greek and Cyrillic print as written: Seán, Dvořák, Ødegård, Nguyễn, Ελένη, Анна. Names in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hebrew or Arabic script can’t be printed yet. We show you which ones before you buy, so you can type a romanized form.
