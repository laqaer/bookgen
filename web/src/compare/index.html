One by one

Where each option is the better choice comes first. If it fits you, use it.

FamilySearch fan chart

Checked 25 September 2026

A free fan chart of 4 to 7 generations, as a PDF, for trees kept on FamilySearch. It can color ancestors by the country they were born in.

The better choice when

  • Your tree is on FamilySearch and you want a free chart now.
  • A PDF of up to 7 generations is all you need.

Where Gildroot is different

  • Works with a file from any program: Ancestry, MyHeritage, RootsMagic and others.
  • Posters up to 24×36 with up to 8 generations, and bowtie and pedigree charts too.
  • Atlas birthplace colors come with a legend and a list of places to assign by hand.

MyHeritage charts

Checked 25 September 2026

Free charts in 18 styles with unlimited PDFs, for trees kept on MyHeritage. Printed posters are available for a fee.

The better choice when

  • Your tree is on MyHeritage and you want a free chart.
  • You want more styles to choose from: 18, where Gildroot has 6.
  • You want the poster printed for you.

Where Gildroot is different

  • Works with trees kept anywhere, not only on one website.
  • Your file is read on your own computer and never uploaded.
  • Charts are typeset for large prints, up to 24×36.

familytreechart.com

Checked 25 September 2026

Charts from a GEDCOM file or FamilySearch, up to 10 generations, sold as digital files, prints or framed prints from $34.95 to $459.95. Selling charts since 2020.

The better choice when

  • You want 9 or 10 generations; Gildroot stops at 8.
  • You want it printed, or printed and framed, for you.
  • You want to bring a tree straight in from FamilySearch.

Where Gildroot is different

  • You design it yourself with a live preview, and see it before paying.
  • Bowtie charts for two families, and Atlas birthplace colors.
  • From ${{site.prices.heirloom}}, with re-exports for good, and nothing uploaded.

FamilyPDF

Checked 25 September 2026

A fan chart of 4 to 6 generations, plus up to 12 children, from a GEDCOM file up to 5 MB. $19.99 once removes the watermark.

The better choice when

  • Price matters most: it costs $9 less than Gildroot’s Heirloom.
  • You want the root person’s children on the fan as well.

Where Gildroot is different

  • Up to 8 generations, 3 chart types and 6 styles.
  • Tiled printing at home, and JPEGs for photo counters.
  • A free 5-generation Letter PDF, with a one-line credit at the foot rather than a watermark.

Family ChartMasters

Checked 25 September 2026

Printed charts with designer proofs and shipping: $30 to $40 for a chart you fill in, $49 self-designed, and $90 to $120 pre-filled.

The better choice when

  • You want to see proofs from a designer before it is printed.
  • You want it printed and shipped as part of the order.

Where Gildroot is different

  • Ready in minutes, not after proofs and shipping.
  • From ${{site.prices.heirloom}}, or free for a Letter PDF.
  • Change it and export again whenever your research grows.

Etsy designers

Checked 25 September 2026

Independent designers who make a custom chart from the names you send, for about $5 to $150, usually in 3 to 10 days. Most show 3 or 4 generations, typed in by hand.

The better choice when

  • You want a one-of-a-kind, hand-designed piece, or an art style Gildroot doesn’t offer.
  • You would like to support an individual artist.

Where Gildroot is different

  • Ready tonight, not in days.
  • Uses your whole file, so no names are retyped or misspelled.
  • You can fix anything yourself and export again.
