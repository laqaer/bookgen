The three programs Terms as of 30 September 2026
1

Affiliates

For bloggers, newsletter writers and anyone with readers who research their family.

  • 30% commission on the first order of each customer you refer.
  • A 30-day window: if someone clicks your link and buys within 30 days, the order counts as yours.
  • Approved automatically when you sign up.
  • Tracking and payouts are handled by Lemon Squeezy’s affiliate program, on its own schedule and terms.
2

Genealogical societies

For state and county societies, lineage-society chapters and family associations with a newsletter or website.

  • A 15% discount code for your members.
  • 20% of each order made with the code goes to the society, through a Lemon Squeezy affiliate account in the society’s own name.
  • Reunion organizers can pair it with the Professional license, and with free blank charts and the Ask-the-family link.
3

Creator kit

For people who make videos, podcasts, webinars and blogs about family history, whether or not you join as an affiliate.

  • A free Professional license, so you can make and show charts for any family.
  • Sample files and brand assets: our logo, product images and plain descriptions.
  • Early access to new chart styles before they are released.

The ground rules

They are the same rules we hold ourselves to. We may end a partnership that breaks them.

  • Say that you earn a commission wherever you share your link or code.
  • Describe Gildroot as it is. Charts are made from the user’s own file; nothing is uploaded; prices are one-time. Please don’t promise features it doesn’t have.
  • No spam. No unsolicited bulk email, no posting your link where a community’s rules forbid it, and no fake reviews or accounts.
  • Your own honest opinion. We never ask for a positive review, and a free license or kit comes with no obligation to post anything.

These terms are a plain-language summary and have not yet had a legal review. Commission rates are set by Gildroot’s owner and may change for future orders; we’ll give notice on this page first.
