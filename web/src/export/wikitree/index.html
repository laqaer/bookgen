Export guide

Download your tree from WikiTree.

WikiTree is one shared, free family tree. Its GEDCOM page lets you download the profiles on your watchlist, or the ancestors or descendants of any one person you are allowed to see.

Steps checked against WikiTree’s own help pages on 30 September 2026. Gildroot is independent and not affiliated with WikiTree.

Steps, on the WikiTree website

From WikiTree’s GEDCOM download page and answers on WikiTree’s own help forum, G2G.

  1. Log in to WikiTree.
  2. Open the My WikiTree menu and choose GEDCOMs. (The download page is at wikitree.com/wiki/Special:DownloadGedcom.)
  3. Choose whose profiles to include: the default takes the profiles on your watchlist. For a chart, enter the WikiTree ID of the person the chart is about, shown on their profile, and ask for their ancestors.
  4. Start the download. WikiTree may take some time to build the file; you can come back to My WikiTree › GEDCOMs later.
  5. Save the file on your computer.

Stuck?

Still stuck after these? Write to support@gildroot.com and say which step. Please don’t attach your file; we never need it.

Some relatives are missing from the file.

WikiTree leaves out private profiles unless you are on their Trusted List. Ask the profile manager to add you, or type those people in by hand in the studio.

How big can the file be?

Answers on WikiTree’s forum give a limit of 10,000 profiles per download. A chart needs far fewer: 255 people fill an 8-generation fan.

I can’t find GEDCOMs in the menu.

Go straight to wikitree.com/wiki/Special:DownloadGedcom while logged in.
