Export guide

Export your tree from Gramps.

Gramps is free, open-source genealogy software, and its Export Assistant saves any tree as a GEDCOM file in five short pages.

Steps checked against Gramps’s own help pages on 30 September 2026. Gildroot is independent and not affiliated with Gramps.

Steps, in Gramps 6.0

From the Gramps 6.0 Wiki Manual, “Manage Family Trees: Exporting data.”

  1. Open the family tree you want (Family Trees › Manage Family Trees…, select it, and click Load Family Tree).
  2. Choose Family Trees › Export…, or press Ctrl+E (⌘+E on a Mac). The Export Assistant opens.
  3. On the first page, “Saving your data”, click Next.
  4. Choose GEDCOM as the output format and click Next.
  5. Check the filters. The defaults export everyone. To keep living relatives’ names, leave the Living filter on Include all selected people. Click Next.
  6. Choose a file name and folder, click Next, and confirm on the last page. Gramps writes the .ged file.

The short version

Family TreesExport…GEDCOMNextNextconfirm

Stuck?

Still stuck after these? Write to support@gildroot.com and say which step. Please don’t attach your file; we never need it.

I only want one person’s ancestors.

On the filters page, set the Person Filter to Ancestors of the active person. Make that person active in Gramps first.

Living relatives came out as “Living”.

The Living filter was set to replace names. Export again with Include all selected people.

Some people are missing.

The Privacy filter, on by default, leaves out records marked private. Untick Do not include records marked private if you want them.

Is GEDCOM a good backup of Gramps?

No. The Gramps manual recommends Gramps XML for backups, because GEDCOM cannot carry everything Gramps stores. For a chart, GEDCOM has all Gildroot needs.
