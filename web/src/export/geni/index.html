Export guide

Export your tree from Geni.

Geni builds the GEDCOM file for you and sends a link to download it. You choose which group of relatives goes in.

Steps checked against Geni’s own help pages on 30 September 2026. Gildroot is independent and not affiliated with Geni.

Steps, on the Geni website

From Geni’s help center article “How can I export my GEDCOM?”

  1. Log in to Geni on a computer.
  2. Click the Family tab, click Share Your Tree, then click Export. (Or open a person’s profile, click Actions, and choose Export GEDCOM.)
  3. Choose which group of people to export. For a chart of one person’s ancestors, an ancestors export is enough; larger groups take longer.
  4. Start the export. Geni generates the file and sends you a link to download it.
  5. Download the file from the link and save it on your computer.

How many people can I export?

Geni’s help page says exports are limited to 4 times the number of profiles you have added, up to 5,000 profiles on a Basic account and 50,000 on a Pro account. A chart needs far fewer: 255 people fill an 8-generation fan.

The link hasn’t arrived.

Large exports take longer to build. Check your spam folder, and check the email address on your Geni account.

Which group should I pick?

Geni offers five kinds of export, each a different set of relatives. For a fan or pedigree chart, ancestors are all you need. For a bowtie, export each partner’s ancestors, or a group that includes both.

The download ends in .zip. Is that right?

Yes. Drop the .zip file into Gildroot as it is; it finds the GEDCOM file inside. You can also unzip it first: double-click it on a Mac, or right-click it and choose Extract All on Windows.
