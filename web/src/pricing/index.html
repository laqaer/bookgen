Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. You pay once and keep the license. There is no subscription and nothing renews. You can export the same chart again, at any size your license covers, as often as you like.

Do I need to pay to see my chart?

No. Every chart, style and color mode works on screen for free, and a Letter or A4 PDF of up to 5 generations in the Ivory style is free to download. You only pay for larger sizes, more generations, the other styles and the other export types.

What counts as “one family” for Heirloom?

One person at the center of the chart, or one couple for a bowtie chart. You can make as many charts of that person or couple as you like, in any chart type, style and size. The key remembers who that is. If you later want a different person at the center, you can move up to Family Historian for ${{site.prices.upgrade}}.

What happens when I get a new computer or tablet?

Paste your key into the studio on the new device. Each device counts as one activation: 3 for Heirloom, 5 for Family Historian, 9 for Family Pack and 10 for Professional. If you run out because you replaced a device, write to us and we’ll reset your activations.

My brother and sister want charts too. Which should we buy?

Family Pack, ${{site.prices.familyPack}}. It gives up to 3 households Family Historian rights, each with their own devices, so everyone can make the charts they want from the same family file.

What if I change my mind?

Write to us within 30 days of buying and we refund you in full, with no questions. Reply to your receipt email or write to {{site.email.support}}.
