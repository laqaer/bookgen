Legal

30 days, no questions.

If a Gildroot license isn’t right for you, ask within 30 days of buying and we refund you in full. You don’t need to give a reason.

Last updated 30 September 2026Related: Terms · License

This is a plain-language draft and has not yet had a legal review.

How to ask

  1. Reply to the receipt email you got when you bought, or write to {{site.email.support}}.
  2. If you write a new email, include the email address you bought with, so we can find the order.
  3. That’s all. We don’t ask why, and we don’t ask you to try anything first.

What is covered

Every paid product, for 30 days from the day you bought it: Heirloom, Family Historian, Family Pack, Professional, the upgrade from Heirloom, and gift keys. A gift key is refunded to the person who paid for it.

What happens next

  • We refund the full amount you paid, through Lemon Squeezy, which ran the checkout, to the card or account you paid with. When it appears on your statement depends on your bank.
  • Your license key is switched off, so it stops unlocking paid exports. The free version keeps working as before.
  • We confirm by email when the refund has been made.

After 30 days

If something is wrong with Gildroot itself, such as a chart that won’t export or a name that won’t print, write to us at any time and we’ll try to fix it. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under consumer protection law.
