Legal

What each license allows.

Every Gildroot license is bought once and never expires. Heirloom, Family Historian and Family Pack are for you and your family. Professional is for charts you make for others or sell.

Last updated 30 September 2026Related: Terms · Refunds · Pricing

Every plan, free or paid

  • Your charts are yours. The names, dates and places on them come from your file. Print them, frame them, give them away and share pictures of them.
  • The software is ours. A license lets you use Gildroot; it doesn’t let you copy, resell or redistribute the software, or share your key with people it doesn’t cover.
  • Fonts inside your PDFs are open-source fonts under the SIL Open Font License, embedded so the chart prints exactly as you saw it.

Free

No license needed. See every chart, style and color mode on screen, and download a Letter or A4 PDF of a fan or pedigree chart of up to 5 generations in the Ivory style, and a 1080×1350 share image. Free exports carry a small “Made with Gildroot” line. For personal and family use.

Heirloom, ${{site.prices.heirloom}}

  • One family: charts centered on one person, or on one couple for a bowtie chart. The key remembers who that is when you first activate it.
  • All 3 chart types, all 6 styles, all 3 color modes, every size up to 24×36 and A1, and every export: PDF, tiled home printing, JPEG and the share image without its mark.
  • Unlimited re-exports, for good.
  • Up to 3 devices.
  • Personal and family use.

Family Historian, ${{site.prices.historian}}

  • Everything in Heirloom, for any number of trees and people at the center.
  • Every chart style and chart type we add later, at no extra cost.
  • Up to 5 devices.
  • Personal and family use.

Family Pack, ${{site.prices.familyPack}}

  • Family Historian rights for up to 3 households, such as a set of brothers and sisters.
  • Up to 9 devices, 3 per household.
  • Personal and family use.

Professional, ${{site.prices.professional}}

  • Everything in Family Historian, for commercial use.
  • Make charts for clients, members or customers, and charge for them: professional genealogists, reunion organizers, genealogical societies, and shops selling printed or digital charts.
  • Add a “Prepared by” line with your business name.
  • Up to 10 devices within your business.

Personal or commercial?

Personal and family use means charts for yourself, your relatives and your friends, including printing copies for a family gathering, as long as nobody pays you for them. If you charge money for charts, sell them, or make them as a service for people outside your family, including a society’s members, that is commercial use, and it needs Professional.

If you aren’t sure which you need, write to {{site.email.hello}} and ask. We’ll give you a straight answer.

Devices and activations

Each browser on each computer, tablet or phone where you paste your key uses one activation. Clearing your browser’s saved data for this site counts as a new device. If you run out because you replaced a device, write to {{site.email.support}} and we’ll reset your activations.

Upgrades and gifts

You can move from Heirloom to Family Historian for ${{site.prices.upgrade}}, the difference in price. Gift keys give the same rights as the license they are for; the rights belong to the person who redeems the key.

When a license ends

A license lasts for good. It ends only if you ask for a refund, or if a key was bought fraudulently or shared publicly in breach of these terms. When a license ends, the key stops unlocking paid exports.
