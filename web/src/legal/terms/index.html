2. What Gildroot is

Gildroot is software that runs in your web browser. It reads a family tree file you choose, or names you type in, and draws a chart from it. The studio and the free tools do this on your own device: your file is not uploaded to us. The privacy page explains exactly what does leave your device.

There is no account to create. You can use the website, see every chart on screen and download the free Letter or A4 PDF without paying or giving us any personal details.

You are responsible for what you choose to put on a chart and share. Please respect living relatives’ privacy: by default the studio hides the dates of people who may still be living.

6. Accuracy

A chart shows what is in your file or what you typed. We work hard to read files faithfully and print every name correctly, but we don’t check anyone’s family history, and we can’t promise that a file from another program contains what you expect. Look over your chart before you print it; you can correct any name, date or place in the studio.

Our sample charts use Queen Victoria’s ancestors from Wikidata (CC0) and a fictional sample family, labeled as such.

To the extent the law allows, if something goes wrong our responsibility to you is limited to the amount you paid us for your license. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under consumer protection law that can’t be excluded.
