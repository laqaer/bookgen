For an anniversary

The two families they joined.

For a 25th or a 50th: both of them in the center with the date they married, and both family trees reaching back behind them.

Why a bowtie for an anniversary

An anniversary celebrates a couple, and a bowtie chart is the one chart with a couple at its center. Their names share the medallion, and under them the wedding date and place, read straight from the marriage record in your file. If the file has no record, you type the date in.

Around them, each partner’s parents, grandparents and great-grandparents, up to 6 generations a side on the largest posters.

A line under the title carries a dedication of your own, such as “For Mom and Dad, 50 years.”

Midnight Gilt, shown here, prints best on photo paper or matte poster paper. For plain paper, choose Ivory or Letterpress.

If you are the children or grandchildren

Often the person with the family tree file is one of the couple, and the gift is meant to be a surprise. Two ways round that:

  • Ask a relative for the file. A cousin or aunt who keeps the tree on Ancestry, MyHeritage or a desktop program can export it in a few minutes. Our export guides walk through each one.
  • Build it from what you know. Type in names, birth years and birthplaces in the studio, then send an Ask-the-family link so aunts and uncles can fill in the rest from their phones. Ask them to keep it a surprise.

Siblings splitting the gift can share one Family Pack license: up to 3 households, $79 once.

A timeline

Counting back from the party.

  1. 6 weeks before: get the file, or start the Quick Builder and send the Ask-the-family link.
  2. 4 weeks before: make the chart. Check the spelling of every name with someone who knew them.
  3. 3 weeks before: order the print online, or plan to print it at a local store. Online printers usually need several business days plus shipping.
  4. 1 week before: frame it. A standard 18×24 or 16×20 frame fits the poster sizes above.
  5. Out of time? The chart itself takes about a minute. A Letter-size copy prints on a home printer tonight, and many drugstore photo counters and office print shops print posters the same day.
