Three charts, three color modes

Which chart?

Every chart is drawn from the same file, in any of six styles. Switching between them in the studio takes one click, so you can look at all three before you decide.

Fan chart One person in the center, ancestors in rings. Up to 8 generations, 255 names. Bowtie chart A couple in the middle, one family on each side. Up to 6 generations a side. Pedigree chart Boxes and lines from left to right, with dates and places. 4 to 6 generations. Atlas birthplace colors Any chart, colored by the country each person was born in.

How many generations fit

The bigger the paper, the more generations it holds. For paper, frames and where to print, see the print planner.
