Changelog

What changed, and when.

Every release of the studio and the site, newest first. Small fixes are grouped by week.

October 2026

Version 1.0

The first release

Charts

  • Three chart types: the fan chart in 180°, 270° and 360° shapes; the bowtie chart of two families meeting in one medallion; and the pedigree chart with boxes and connecting lines.
  • Six styles: Ivory, Midnight Gilt, Botanical, Letterpress, Nordic and Cartographer.
  • Three ways to color a chart: by the style’s own tones, by family line, or by birthplace with Atlas, which shows a legend and how many birthplaces it placed.
  • Sizes from Letter and A4 to 24×36 and A1, with up to 8 generations on the largest.
  • A keepsake layout for trees of 4 generations or fewer: larger type, full dates and places, and an ornamental frame.
  • Pedigree collapse marked with a small number and a legend line; living people’s dates hidden by default.

Your file

  • Reads GEDCOM 5.5, 5.5.1 and 7.0 files, .zip files with a GEDCOM inside, and GEDCOM 7 .gdz files.
  • Reads UTF-8, UTF-16, ANSEL and the older Windows, DOS and Mac character sets, so accented names come through intact.
  • Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. How that works.
  • No file? The Quick Builder takes up to 15 people, and an Ask-the-family link lets a relative fill in the rest from their phone.
  • Fix any name, date or place on the chart without changing your file.

Exports

  • Vector PDF at the exact paper size, with optional bleed and crop marks for print shops.
  • Tiled home printing across Letter or A4 sheets, with crop marks and an assembly map.
  • JPEGs for photo counters, and a 1080×1350 image for sharing.
  • Free: a Letter or A4 PDF of up to 5 generations in the Ivory style.

On the site

  • One-time licenses, gift keys with a printable gift card, and a 30-day refund.
  • Guides to printing an Ancestry tree, occasions, and help.
  • Free tools: a cousin calculator, a private GEDCOM viewer and an ancestor calculator.
