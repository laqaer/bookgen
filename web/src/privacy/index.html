Privacy

Your family tree stays on this computer.

Gildroot reads your file, draws your chart and makes your PDF inside your own web browser. Your tree is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No upload. Names, dates, places and the file itself stay on your device.
  • No cookies. Our pages set none.
  • Counts, not people. Our analytics see “7 generations”, never a name.

How it works Checked by an automated test before every release

Nothing is uploaded.

  1. When you open the studio, your browser downloads Gildroot’s program from gildroot.com, the same way it downloads any web page.
  2. When you drop your file, that program reads it right there in the browser. The file is not sent anywhere.
  3. The chart is drawn, and the PDF or JPEG is made, on this computer. The download goes straight to your own Downloads folder.

What our analytics count

We use Plausible Analytics to learn which pages help people and where the studio gets stuck. Plausible uses no cookies and, by its own published policy, never stores IP addresses: it turns them into a visitor code that is deleted every 24 hours.

For each page view, Plausible records the page address (never the part after a # sign), the site that linked you here, your browser, operating system and device type, and your country.

In the studio, a few events are counted, such as “tree opened”, “export” and “checkout”. Each can carry only these details, and anything else is dropped before it is sent:

  • chart type, style and color mode, such as “fan”, “ivory”, “atlas”;
  • number of generations and paper size, such as “7” and “18×24”;
  • how many people are in the file, as a range: under 100, 100 to 1,000, 1,000 to 5,000, 5,000 to 20,000, or over 20,000;
  • the kind of export, whether you started from your own file or a sample, and your license tier.

That is the complete list. A name, a date, a place or a file name is never among them.

What is saved on your device

So you can close the tab and pick up later, the studio saves your project in your browser’s own storage on this device, and remembers your license key there. Nothing is copied to us. To remove it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

No cookies

Gildroot’s pages set no cookies, for advertising or anything else. Lemon Squeezy’s checkout page, which opens in its own tab when you buy, has its own policy.

No third-party scripts in the studio

The studio and free tools run only Gildroot’s own code. A security rule built into those pages tells your browser to refuse connections to anywhere except gildroot.com, Lemon Squeezy’s license service and Plausible.

Check it yourself: try it with wifi off

You don’t have to take our word for it. Once the studio has everything it needs, it keeps working with no internet connection at all.

  1. Open the studio, try the sample family and download its free PDF once, so every part of the program has loaded.
  2. Turn off wifi, or unplug the network cable.
  3. Drop your file, build your chart, and download the free Letter PDF.
  4. Turn wifi back on. Nothing was sent, because there was nowhere to send it.

Buying and checking a license key need a connection. A key that was checked in the last 30 days keeps working offline.

If you know your way around a browser’s developer tools, the Network tab shows every request the page makes. You won’t find your file in any of them.
